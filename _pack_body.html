How to use this pack

One pack, five readers. Each reader needs two or three sections; the rest is context. Everything here follows the shoot plan both sides agreed: 7 September full day and 8 September morning at the 1688 Hangzhou campus, English on camera, Ary on camera for one afternoon.

Ary · 1688

Aria · WorldFirst
Sections 03, 05, 06 and 07. The order of the Q&A hour, her link lines on the walk, the WorldFirst side of the mutual credit, and the host sheet.
Sean and Alan · crew

1688 · via Aria

Steven · WorldFirst
All of it. On site, off camera.
Rules of the day
  • English on camera. Chinese prompting off camera is fine; the take then restarts in English from the top.
  • One question, one take. Every answer stands alone. No "as I said earlier", no reference to another answer, no client, customer or buyer named. If it happens, the take goes again.
  • Framed safe for 9:16: subject centred, nothing essential at the sides of the frame.
  • Every Q&A answer ends on the same line: "And when it's time to pay, you can pay direct." It is the last line of every answer sheet.
  • On-claim wording only. The payment route is "the official cross-border payment solution from 1688 and WorldFirst". Never "the only".
  • Numbers Ary says on camera come from 1688. Where a sheet shows a bracket, 1688 fills it before the day; if a bracket stays empty, the line is cut, not guessed.
  • The brand is written WorldFirst. Never WORLDFIRST, never World First.

Aria's host sheet

How to run the seat and the walk so that every take is usable on its own. Aria reads this once before the day and keeps it beside the lens.

Before cameras roll · lunch
  • Walk Ary through the afternoon in this order: testimonial, Q&A, walk. Show him the prompter and let him read Version A once, off camera, at his own pace.
  • Agree the restart cue in Chinese: "我们再来一次，从头。" He restarts from the first line, in English, without explaining.
  • Tell him the two things that force a retake: naming a client or company, and referring to another answer. Everything else is fixable in the edit.
  • Agree which brackets in section 04 are filled and which lines are cut. Cross the cut lines out on his printed set.
In the seat
  • Ask each question once, in the words on the sheet, then silence. No "mm-hm", no verbal nods; they land on his audio track.
  • Count two seconds after his last word before you speak, so every take has a clean tail.
  • If he stumbles, let him finish, then take it again from the top. A take that starts mid-answer cannot be used alone.
  • Chinese prompting between takes is fine. The take itself restarts in English from line one.
  • If he forgets the payment line, ask for it alone as a pickup: "Just the last line, please."
  • The crew voice-slates every take with the pack ID and take number ("B5, take 2"). You confirm the ID before each question.
  • Sit off-axis so Ary looks at you, not the lens. The testimonial is the exception: to lens, and you sit directly beneath it.
  • The clock: start 14:30; checkpoint 15:10; if fewer than twelve takes are done, jump to E6, E7 and F1. Ary is released at 16:30 whatever is left.
On the walk
  • You carry the links; Ary carries one or two lines per leg. If he is tired or hot, drop questions and keep pictures. The seat already has the words.
  • Reset points are at the start of each leg. Sound checks there, not mid-leg.
  • Stop if 1688's minder objects to a location or a screen. Nothing on the walk is worth a permission argument.
Never on camera
  • Client, customer or buyer names. Confidential 1688 numbers unless Ary offers them. Personal data on screens. "The only" in any wording about payment.
