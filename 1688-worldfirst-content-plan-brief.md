# 1688 × WorldFirst campaign, content plan

Steven · 1 September 2026 · draft for discussion

WorldFirst and 1688 are co-producing a video campaign for Southeast Asian business buyers, starting with Malaysia, Vietnam and Thailand. All content comes from three shoot events: a walk-through of the 1688 campus in Hangzhou, one seated interview with a 1688 representative, and filming with three customers in their own markets. This document lists the five content tracks, what each one produces, and what the shoot needs to capture.

Rules that apply to all tracks: English on camera, subtitled into local languages. No customer is named in any campus footage, so that footage remains usable regardless of casting.

## Outcome map

| Track | Output | Format | Job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. What is 1688 | 1 film | 8 to 10 min | Introduce the platform to buyers who do not know it |
| 2. Comment Q&A | 8 to 10 reels, plus the full interview as one long-form video | Vertical, 30 to 60s each | Answer the questions buyers already ask |
| 3. Market mini-series | 1 episode per market | 90s each | Show what each market sources from 1688 |
| 4. Joint testimonial | 3 customer films | 3 to 5 min each | Customer proof, with both companies on camera |
| 5. Product mini-run | 3 to 4 screen-demo episodes, plus 1 roadmap episode | 60 to 90s each | Show how to use the platform, current updates, and what is coming |

## The five tracks

### 1. What is 1688

A single film that explains the platform: what 1688 is, why prices are lower than on international sites, its history since 1999, and the scale of the supply chain behind it (100M+ annual active buyers). The campus walk-through provides the visuals for this film. Existing videos comparing 1688 with Alibaba.com have 3.68M views; none of them addresses Southeast Asian buyers in English. This film fills that gap.

### 2. Comment Q&A

An interview between a WorldFirst host and a 1688 representative, shot at a table in podcast format. Each question is answered in one self-contained take, framed for vertical cutting. Each reel opens with the actual question as buyers phrase it online, then the answer.

The question list comes from the audience research: how to pay from outside China, whether an agent is needed, which countries are eligible, landed cost and duty, and the difference from Alibaba.com. Payment appears in two forms: each reel ends with one line confirming that direct payment is available, and dedicated takes cover how 1688 works with WorldFirst.

### 3. Market mini-series

Short episodes in which the 1688 representative and WorldFirst discuss one market at a time: what buyers there source, top categories, and regional differences. This data is not available on camera anywhere else. Two conditions: only markets where direct payment is currently available are featured, and the episodes are recorded as blocks within the same seated interview.

### 4. Joint testimonial

Three customers, one per market, filmed at their own business. Each film contains three parts:

- The customer describes how they source on 1688 and pay through WorldFirst, including what their previous route cost and what the current one costs. Cost figures are stated by the customer, not by either company.
- The 1688 representative confirms that overseas business buyers are supported on the platform.
- The WorldFirst representative explains how the payment itself works in that market.

### 5. Product mini-run

Screen-recorded demonstrations by the 1688 representative: how to use the platform effectively, features that experienced buyers use, and recent platform updates. One additional episode covers the platform's plans for Southeast Asia. This roadmap episode is also usable by both companies' communications teams, and has a reserved slot in the shoot schedule.

## How the shoot covers it

- The campus walk-through produces track 1.
- The seated interview produces tracks 2, 3 and 5, recorded as separate blocks.
- The customer filming days produce track 4, with the 1688 and WorldFirst statements added from the interview and the Singapore session.

A single question bank covering all interview blocks (buyer questions, market blocks, product demonstrations, roadmap, and each company's description of the other) is in preparation this week.

## Constraint

The 1688 representative's confirmed on-camera time is about 2.5 hours. Tracks 2, 3 and 5 all draw on this session. Covering all three at full depth requires approximately one additional hour, which needs to be requested before the shoot date is fixed.
